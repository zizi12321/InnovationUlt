Data and artwork sources

Innovation Ultimate is designed by Carl Chudyk and published by Asmadi Games.
Card text and artwork remain the property of their respective rights holders.
Official reference: https://www.asmadigames.com/innovation/

This project's card data and Chinese translations were adapted from the
previously reviewed fourth-edition catalogue. Sources are also recorded in
data/base-cards.json, data/echoes-cards.json, data/figures-cards.json, data/cities-cards.json and individual card records.
BGA implementation and image atlas:
https://github.com/micahstairs/bga-innovation

Echoes of the Past text and rule cross-checks:
https://www.asmadigames.com/rules/InnoUlt_Rulebook_v4.1.pdf (pp. 21, 24-25)
https://www.asmadigames.com/innovation/ (cards E001-E115)
https://github.com/micahstairs/bga-innovation/tree/9b5464e6c9382d587e229741aeaa2de932e4ed8e/modules/Innovation/Cards/Echoes
The local echoes-illustrations.png is the Echoes row extracted from the existing
BGA image atlas. Rules are explicit JavaScript implementations; the cited BGA
fourth-edition code was used to resolve specific ordering and boundary cases.

Figures in the Sand text and rule cross-checks:
https://www.asmadigames.com/rules/InnoUlt_Rulebook_v4.1.pdf (pp. 21, 28-30)
https://www.asmadigames.com/innovation/ (F001-F115, Z001-Z015)
Figure illustrations are cropped from the existing BGA atlas; age-zero images
and the Food icon come from the previously reviewed catalogue sources. The
standalone catalogue is unchanged. English paragraphs and numeric boxes in
the game data are aligned with downloaded official card pages.

Cities of Destiny references:
https://www.asmadigames.com/rules/InnoUlt_Rulebook_v4.1.pdf (pp. 9-10, 21-23)
https://www.asmadigames.com/innovation/ (official C001-C115 card images)
https://github.com/micahstairs/bga-innovation/tree/9b5464e6c9382d587e229741aeaa2de932e4ed8e
Chinese city names and six-position data derive from the reviewed BGA catalogue.
Cities contain printed icons rather than paragraphs: the bilingual explanations
in game data are paraphrases of the official rules. cities-illustrations.png is
the Cities row of the existing BGA atlas. Special symbols and magnifiers use
local SVG paths. No official full-card images are shipped or loaded at runtime.

Rule terminology references klingeling's Yucata localization project:
https://code.915159.xyz/yucata/InnovationUltimate.json
The license below applies to that localization contribution, not to the
underlying game or all card text and artwork.

Artifacts of History: 115 card faces/effect texts and illustration row from the same official Ultimate/BGA sources above. Chinese rule corrections checked against official individual card pages and rulebook v4.1 pp.26–27. Five Museum cards are represented by a locally authored generic museum symbol.

The Unseen: 115 cards and five special achievements from the same official
Ultimate card pages and reviewed BGA catalogue. Rules checked against v4.1
pp.13, 21 and 31, including the Robber Barons and Teleprompter appendices.
unseen-illustrations.png is the Unseen row of the existing BGA illustration
atlas. The Safe lock symbol is locally authored SVG. All assets are local;
the application does not contact BGA, Asmadi or an AI service during play.
